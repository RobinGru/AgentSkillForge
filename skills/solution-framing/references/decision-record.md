# Decision record fields

Use this guide to make the decision brief reviewable.

## Evidence status

- **Observed:** directly seen in the repository, runtime, or supplied artifact.
- **Provided:** explicitly stated by the requester or an authoritative source.
- **Assumed:** needed to proceed but not established; state how it can be
  checked.
- **Unknown:** missing information that has not been inferred.

## Field guidance

| Brief field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Decision | The question being resolved and the selected result. |
| Evidence | Facts and their status; include source or location when available. |
| Assumptions | Unverified premises and their potential effect. |
| Boundaries | Scope, exclusions, constraints, interfaces, and owners. |
| Selected approach | Why this direction meets the decision criteria. |
| Rejected approaches | Each viable alternative and its rejection reason. |
| Risks and mitigations | Credible failure modes and proportionate responses. |
| Acceptance evidence | Checks, artifacts, or observations that demonstrate success. |
| Open blockers | Facts or approvals that still prevent the stated handoff. |
| Handoff state | One permitted outcome and the immediate next action. |
