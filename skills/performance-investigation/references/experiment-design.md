# Performance experiment design

A useful experiment eliminates at least one plausible explanation with minimal
change and comparable measurement conditions.

## Experiment record

```markdown
## Hypothesis

## Supporting evidence

## Smallest discriminating experiment

## Expected result

## Actual result

## Decision
```

State control and treatment, fixed conditions, workload or sample, primary
metric, side metrics, and stopping condition. Change one material variable at a
time. If production experimentation has user, reliability, privacy, or cost
risk, prefer a safe representative environment or use explicit approval and
rollback conditions.

An inconclusive result is evidence: document it, retain the baseline, and choose
the next observation rather than broadening a change until a metric moves.
